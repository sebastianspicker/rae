# CLM-021 Negative Results

## Claim statement

Negative results should be preserved as first-class evidence when they constrain
interpretation, calibration, or future design.

## Claim class

`governance_rule`

## Proof mode

Research integrity and reproducibility doctrine.

## Assumptions

- discarded failures distort both future design and public interpretation
- null or adverse outcomes can still teach calibration, scoping, and artifact
  design lessons
- publication incentives otherwise bias the surviving record

Expected home is the result ledger plus run and benchmark cards.

## Counterarguments

- exhaustive preservation can create noise when failures are trivial or
  unreproducible
- operational teams may need lightweight triage before deciding what to retain

## Validity threats

- a recorded negative result can still be misleading if the setup is incomplete
- poor taxonomy can make a negative result hard to interpret later

## Review status

Adopted as a publication rule.
